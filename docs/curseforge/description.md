## 🆕 Latest Update (v2.2.0) — Seals, Ultimate & Stability

**Command Seals** — 3 charges per day (they recharge at the start of a new day or after sleeping):
- **Overdrive** (J · 100 mana): Strength, Resistance and Speed for your hero for 30 seconds. When it ends, your hero falls and can't be revived until it fully recovers.
- **Revive** (K · 150 mana): brings a defeated hero back at full health.
- **Heal** (V · 50 mana): fully heals your summoned hero.

**Ultimate — Explosion** (U · 200 mana): your hero channels for a moment and then explodes, damaging, burning and knocking back nearby enemies. Once per day.

- Your hero (or its spirit) now always comes back to you after teleports, ender pearls, respawns, dimension changes and logging in
- New `/althera_hero unstuck` command to fix a stuck summon
- Commands renamed to `/althera_hero ...` and `/althera_mana ...`
- Big batch of stability fixes, including a server crash when Overdrive ended

### Temporary Limitation
For now, hero availability has been intentionally limited while the new animation/model pipeline is stabilized. Aria (EX Rank) is currently the main playable showcase hero, featuring custom visuals, animations, and combat behavior, although the system itself is still actively being refined and expanded.

## ⚔️ Althera — A Fate-Inspired Progression Mod
Althera is a progression-driven mod focused on summoning and growth. Your companion evolves alongside you — gaining levels, scaling with your power, and becoming a core part of your journey.

As you play, you develop mana, abilities, and combat potential. Progression is continuous, shaping both your character and your summon.

## 🎮 How to Play
- Press **H** to summon your companion
- Press **G** to open the status screen
- Use your **Command Seals**: **J** Overdrive · **K** Revive · **V** Heal
- Press **U** to unleash your hero's **Ultimate**

Your summon consumes mana while active and gains experience by defeating mobs.
When dismissed, it enters spirit form, regenerates faster, and grants Resistance I.

All keys can be changed in Options → Controls (Althera category).
If your summon ever gets stuck, use `/althera_hero unstuck`.

Manage your mana and evolve your summon over time.
