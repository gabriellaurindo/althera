# Heroes

Every hero you can summon in Althera (5 today). Pick your first one on the **Choose your Hero** screen; the [Ritual System](Ritual-System) swaps it for another later.

Classes follow a cycle: each one is strong against two others (+25% damage) and weak against two (-25%). See [Core Systems](Core-Systems) and [Command Seals & Ultimates](Command-Seals-and-Ultimates).

> **Note:** Hero models, textures and animations are work in progress and will change between versions.

---

## Aria

<p align="center"><img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2065/934/2026-10-17_01-50-05-jpg.jpg" alt="Aria" width="320"></p>

*A graceful swordswoman of the old order, known for her unwavering discipline and elegant blade technique.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rarity** | EX |
| **Class** | Saber |
| **Strong against** | Caster, Lancer |
| **Weak against** | Archer, Assassin |
| **Combat style** | Melee |
| **Personality** | Honorable and calm |

**⚔️ Signature attack:** **Judgment**: her strikes deal bonus damage to hostile mobs, and even more to the undead.

**💥 Ultimate: Explosion**: Your hero stands still and gathers power for 1 second, then explodes: every creature within 6 blocks takes heavy damage, is set on fire and is knocked back. You are never hit.

<p align="center"><img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2065/933/2026-10-17_01-51-21-jpg.jpg" alt="Clare" width="320"></p>

*A silent blade in crimson and black who strikes from the shadows and is gone before the echo fades.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rarity** | EX |
| **Class** | Assassin |
| **Strong against** | Saber, Caster |
| **Weak against** | Archer, Shielder |
| **Combat style** | Melee |
| **Personality** | Cold, quiet and loyal |

**⚔️ Signature attack:** **Wither**: every hit inflicts Wither I for 3 seconds.

**💥 Ultimate: Crimson Requiem**: Clare stands still over a crimson mist for 1.5 seconds, then dashes 8 blocks towards where you are looking, stopping before walls. Everything in her path takes heavy damage and Wither II. Aim while she channels.

*A stalwart shield maiden who plants her tower shield and lets nothing past.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rarity** | S |
| **Class** | Shielder |
| **Strong against** | Archer, Assassin |
| **Weak against** | Caster, Lancer |
| **Combat style** | Melee |
| **Personality** | Calm and unyielding |

**⚔️ Signature attack:** **Shield Bash**: every bash knocks her target back.

**💥 Ultimate: Aegis Dome**: Irisa hurls her shield into the air for about 1 second, then raises a blue dome around her: every enemy within 5 blocks is pushed out, and she and you gain Resistance II and Absorption II for 10 seconds.
