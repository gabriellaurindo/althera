# Origin of the Name

The name **Althera** was created by me (Darksune / gabriellaurindo) specifically for this project, and it carries two
meanings tied to each other.

---

## 1. The core mechanic: to alter

The idea behind the mod is to let the player **alter** what they summon. The player is a summoner: they summon a
creature and can freely modify it, swapping arms, legs, skin and the model itself. The name comes from **"alterar"**
(Portuguese for "to alter"), the action that defines the mod.

---

## 2. The lore: the goddess Althera

Within the story, **Althera** is the name of the goddess of the game's world, the source of the power to summon and
transform. The mechanic and the deity share the same name on purpose: the power to alter creatures comes from her.

---

## History

- **February 8, 2026**: development started (first commit in the repository)

- **October 23, 2026**: this explanation was recorded in the wiki
