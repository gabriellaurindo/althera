**Althera 4.3.0 — Ritual Rework** turns the hero ritual into a real summoning: a glowing circle, pedestals that hold your offerings, a three-second channel and Hero Catalysts that let you pick the class and rank of your next hero.

## ✨ What's new

**Summoning circle** — the Ritual Core now draws a glowing circle on the ground. Place a **Ritual Pedestal** on each of its four points (two blocks from the core, one per side) and keep the rest of the circle clear: each point lights up once it holds a pedestal, and the whole circle pulses when the structure is complete.

**Ritual Pedestal** — a new block that holds a single item, floating and turning above it. Right-click with an item to place it, right-click again to take it back. Crafted from four deepslate blocks (polished, bricks or tiles), two gold ingots and an amethyst shard.

**Offerings and channel** — put a diamond block on a pedestal and right-click the core to roll a new hero. Every ritual costs **300 mana**, so your max mana must have grown past 300 first. The ritual channels for **three seconds** while the offerings rise into the core, ending in a burst of light. Stay within 8 blocks: walking away, taking an offering back or breaking a pedestal interrupts it at no cost, and every failure tells you what is missing.

**Hero Catalyst** — High Nobles (red) now also drop a catalyst holding the **class and rank** of the hero that served them. Offer it on a pedestal next to the diamond block to guarantee a hero of that class and rank. Each class has its own icon, and the name shows in the rank's colour.

**New ritual art** — the Ritual Core is a stepped altar of dark stone with gold trim and amethyst runes, crowned by a floating crystal that rises and grows while the ritual channels. Pedestals share the same look.

## 🔧 Changes
- The old structure (gold blocks, netherrack and the diamond block under the core) is no longer used: break it and place the pedestals instead. Ritual Cores placed before this update may need to be broken and placed again to show the circle
- New Ritual Core recipe: an amethyst block on top of two gold ingots around an obsidian, over three deepslate blocks
- `/althera_hero catalyst <class> <rank> [player]` (cheats) gives a catalyst; the creative tab has one EX catalyst per class
- Smaller download: textures recompressed without any visual change, cutting the jar by about a third

⚠️ New blocks and items: clients and servers must both run 4.3.0.
