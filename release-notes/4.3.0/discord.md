## 🔮 Althera 4.3.0 — Ritual Rework is out!

The hero ritual becomes a real summoning, and you can now choose what answers it.

⭕ **Summoning circle**
The Ritual Core draws a glowing circle on the ground. Place a **Ritual Pedestal** on each of its four points (two blocks away, one per side) and keep the circle clear: each point lights up, and the circle pulses when complete.

🏛️ **Ritual Pedestal**
New block that holds one floating item. Right-click to place an offering, again to take it back. Crafted from deepslate, gold ingots and an amethyst shard.

✨ **Offerings and channel**
Diamond block on a pedestal + right-click the core = a new hero. Each ritual costs **300 mana** and channels for **three seconds** while the offerings rise into the core. Walking away or touching an offering interrupts it at no cost, and every failure tells you what is missing.

💎 **Hero Catalyst**
High Nobles (red) now drop a catalyst holding the **class and rank** of their hero. Offer it next to the diamond block to guarantee a hero of that class and rank.

🎨 **New ritual art**
A stepped altar of dark stone, gold and amethyst runes, crowned by a floating crystal that rises while the ritual channels.

**Also:** old ritual structures no longer work, place the pedestals instead (old cores may need to be placed again) · new Ritual Core recipe · jar about a third smaller

⚠️ Clients and servers must both run 4.3.0.
