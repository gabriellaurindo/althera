## 🦸 Althera 3.0.0 — Choose your Hero is out!

🎴 **Choose your Hero**: no more random first hero, pick the one you want on a new selection screen (existing players can switch once, keeping their level!)
🗡️ **Clare** joins as an EX hero of the new **Assassin** class: fast, deadly and fragile
👼 **Aria reborn**: brand new model and animations, with golden wings during her ultimate
📊 **Skill HUD** next to the hotbar with timers, cooldowns and seal charges
💙 New mana bar, new hero screen, heroes now player-sized
🔁 The ritual now swaps your hero and keeps your level
