## ⚔️ Althera 4.0.0 — Data-Driven Combat is out!

🔥 **Zaya** joins as an EX **Caster**: fire bolts from a distance, Explosion as her Ultimate
🔱 **Valka** joins as an EX **Lancer**: a spear that pierces through her target
🗡️ **Signature attacks** for every hero, and **Crimson Requiem**, Clare's own Ultimate
🔄 **Class advantages** in hero vs hero fights (+25% / −25%), plus dodge and knockback resistance
🕶️ **Disguise**: hold **H** to summon your hero as a hooded figure nobody can identify
📈 **Faster leveling**, armor per level and a max level of 50
💙 Running dry on mana now locks summoning until half your mana is back
