## 🔍 Althera 4.2.0 — Hero Insight is out!

See exactly what your hero is doing, and Valka gets an Ultimate of her own.

⏱️ **Skill timers**
Every running Command Seal or Ultimate shows a small square under the hero HUD with the seconds left: green while active, red on cooldown. Move them next to the crosshair in the hero screen gear tab (**G**).

📊 **Live hero stats**
The hero screen (**G**) shows your summoned hero's current health, damage, armor, speed and resistance with every buff and debuff applied: green above normal, red below. Active effects show as icons on its portrait.

📋 **Hero details panel**
Click the arrow on the right edge of the hero screen for knockback resistance, dodge, regen, seal charges left today, Ultimate status and class matchups.

⚙️ **All settings in one place**
The gear tab now holds interventions, Hide HUD, HUD corner, offsets and the timers position, with a **Reset** link. The Open Config key is gone.

🗡️ **Ultimate on the HUD**
A golden sword beside your hero's portrait, faded once used. A chime and a chat message tell you when a new day restores it.

🎨 **Creative & datapacks**
Unlimited seals and Ultimate in creative; hero JSONs accept custom `sounds` and `hitbox`.

**Also:** seals are cast only through the wheel (hold **R**; J/K/V and the hotbar slots were removed) · disguised heroes keep 75% speed
**Fixes:** Valka no longer inflicts Wither, her new **Tempest Charge** is a teal charge with lancer knockback · Ultimate key name in Controls

⚠️ Clients and servers must both run 4.2.0.
