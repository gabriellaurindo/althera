**Althera** is a Fate-inspired progression mod: summon an anime hero who fights at your side, levels up with you and grows into the core of your journey. Each hero has a class, a signature attack and an Ultimate, all powered by the mana you build as you play.

## ✨ Features
- 🌟 **Hero summoning**: pick your first hero and call them with a single key
- ⚔️ **Classes**: Saber, Lancer, Caster, Assassin, each strong against some and weak against others
- 📈 **Progression**: heroes gain XP from your fights and level up to **50**
- 💥 **Ultimates**: one devastating skill per hero, once per day
- 🔱 **Command Seals**: Overdrive, Revive and Heal, three charges a day
- 👑 **Nobles**: enemy mages with heroes of their own, guarding shrines across the Overworld
- 📦 **Datapack support**: heroes, classes, attacks, Ultimates and enemy mages are all defined in JSON

## 🎮 How to Play
- On your first login, choose your hero on the **Choose your Hero** screen
- Press **H** to summon your companion (hold **H** to summon it disguised)
- Press **G** to open the hero screen: live stats, a details panel and every setting (gear tab)
- Tap **R** to mark a target for your hero; hold **R** to open the **Command Seal wheel** and cast Overdrive, Revive or Heal
- Press **U** to unleash your hero's **Ultimate**

Your summon consumes mana while active and gains experience by defeating mobs. If it runs out of mana, wait until half your mana is back to summon it again.
When dismissed, it enters spirit form, regenerates faster, and grants Resistance I.

All keys can be changed in Options → Controls (Althera category).
If your summon ever gets stuck, use `/althera_hero unstuck`.
