**Althera 4.2.0 — Hero Insight** lets you see exactly what your hero is doing: running skill timers, live stats with every buff and debuff, a details panel with class matchups, and every HUD option in one place. Valka also gets an Ultimate of her own.

## ✨ What's new

**Skill timers** — every running Command Seal or Ultimate shows a small square under the hero HUD with its icon and the seconds left: green while active, red while on cooldown. Prefer them next to the crosshair? Change it in the hero screen gear tab (**G**).

**Live hero stats** — while your hero is summoned, the hero screen (**G**) shows its current health, damage, armor, speed and damage resistance with every buff and debuff applied (Overdrive, potions...): green when above normal, red when below. Its active effects show as icons on its portrait.

**Hero details panel** — the arrow on the right edge of the hero screen opens a side panel with knockback resistance, dodge, regeneration, Command Seal charges left today, whether the Ultimate is ready, the class multipliers and which classes your hero is strong and weak against.

**All settings in the gear tab** — Disable interventions, Hide HUD, HUD corner, offsets and skill timers position, with a description on hover and a **Reset** link for the HUD position.

**Ultimate on the HUD** — a golden sword beside your hero's portrait, faded once used today. When a new day restores it, a chime and a chat message let you know.

**Creative mode** — Command Seals and the Ultimate are unlimited.

**For datapack creators** — hero JSONs accept custom `sounds` (hurt, death, step) and `hitbox` (width, height).

## 🔧 Changes
- Command Seals are cast only through the Command Seal wheel (hold **R**); the J/K/V shortcuts and the skill slots next to the hotbar were removed
- The crosshair hides while the seal wheel is open
- The Open Config key was removed: options live in the hero screen gear tab (**G**)
- A disguised hero keeps 75% of its speed (was 50%)

## 🐛 Fixes
- Valka no longer inflicts Wither: her new Ultimate, **Tempest Charge**, is a teal 8-block charge with a lancer's knockback
- The Ultimate key showed as "Explosion" in the Controls menu

⚠️ Clients and servers must both run 4.2.0.
