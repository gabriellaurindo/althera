## 👑 Althera 4.1.0 — The Nobles Rise is out!

🧙 **The Noble**: a mini boss mage bound to his own EX hero, with Command Seals, an Ultimate and diamond loot
🏛️ **Noble shrines** across the Overworld, each guarded by a Noble who returns a day after he falls
🔴 **Seal key (R)**: tap to mark your hero's target, hold for the **Command Seal wheel**
✨ **Seal tattoo** on your hand that glows when you cast a seal or Ultimate
⚡ **Hero blink**: melee heroes reach targets hiding on pillars, in water or in boats
📈 Much stronger heroes at high levels, Ultimates that scale with attack and a **new hero HUD**
