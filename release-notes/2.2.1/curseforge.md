**Althera 2.2.1 — License Update** is a small maintenance release. No gameplay changes.

## 🔧 Changes

  - **Artwork** (textures, models, animations): CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — credit required, no commercial use

- Videos, streams and screenshots showing the mod are allowed, including monetized ones.
- Versions up to 2.2.0 stay under MIT.
