## ⚔️ Althera 2.2.0 — Seals, Ultimate & Stability is out!

Your hero just got its first active abilities:

🔱 **Command Seals** — 3 charges per day
• **Overdrive** (J): Strength, Resistance & Speed for 30s… but your hero falls when it ends
• **Revive** (K): bring a defeated hero back at full health
• **Heal** (V): fully heal your hero

💥 **Ultimate — Explosion** (U): your hero channels and blows up nearby enemies. Once per day!

🧭 Your hero now always finds you after teleports, respawns and dimension changes
🛠️ New `/althera_hero unstuck` command + a big batch of stability fixes (including a server crash)
