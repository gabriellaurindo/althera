## What counts as artwork

Everything under `src/main/resources/assets/althera/` except `lang/`, including:

- `textures/` — textures and GUI images
- `geo/` — GeckoLib models
- `animations/` — GeckoLib animations
- `models/` and `blockstates/` — block and item models
